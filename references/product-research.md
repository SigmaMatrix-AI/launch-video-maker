# Product research: find usable content on the official website

Use this before setting the direction in stage one; the template route also uses this method. A quick inspection during route selection only helps choose the route. When valid research exists, the task is explicitly limited to screenshots/recordings, or there is no website, use the existing materials without browsing again.

## Understand the whole product, then explore candidate content

Browse the homepage's main sections and navigation, scroll below the fold, and open tabs, carousels, or collapsed items that affect understanding. First establish what capabilities, assets, and examples exist, then choose feature pages, examples, or official demos relevant to the film's topic. A full-page screenshot preserves layout but does not prove that interactive states, delayed content, or subpages were inspected.

Let production questions guide research: What does this capability actually change? Which input and result are easiest to understand? Which images, illustrations, recordings, brand fonts, or representative examples on the website can be used directly? If there is only a name and no demo, record the capability description and visual gap; do not infer specific UI or successful operation states.

Assets include more than interface screenshots. Relevant website illustrations, product outputs, and real scenarios can also be candidates. Choose materials relevant to the content, preserving their meaning, source, and sufficient clarity. Once the direction is set, capture precisely for the shots needed rather than downloading the whole website in advance.

## Bring research directly into production

Keep a short table in `records/brief.md` documenting the basis for topic selection:

| Content / use | Source | Evidence type / observation | Usable asset or state | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current candidate capability or visual | URL, page section, or local file | Clearly distinguish official claims, directly observed demos, and creative inferences | Corresponding screenshot, recording, illustration, input, or result | UI, states, or high-resolution assets not yet obtained |

Save used assets inside the film project with their sources and any necessary capture state and time. Long formal research may be split into `records/research.md`, leaving conclusions and links in the brief; short tasks need no new file. See [Stage deliverables](stage-deliverables.md#integration-with-existing-review) to include optional files in review.

**Feature names can directly organize the storyboard.** "Search → summarize → share," a single-feature launch, an output showcase, and a continuing example are all valid. Research helps select the right features, understand details, and find expressive materials; it does not require forcing every film into a complex story about the same character. Capabilities, states, and results shown still need evidence.

## When research is sufficient to proceed

Move into creation when the main relevant capabilities and asset locations are clear, the selected content has traceable evidence, key visuals have available assets or explicit illustrative/alternative approaches, and remaining gaps will not force you to invent core capabilities. There is no fixed page or screenshot quota. If stage-two topic selection or stage-three shots raise new questions, research only the relevant parts.

| Good | Bad |
| --- | --- |
| Survey the homepage, then inspect subpages and demos for candidate capabilities, recording available assets | Read only the first-screen slogan and claim to have researched the product |
| Click examples relevant to the topic and distinguish real interaction from marketing descriptions | Substitute a fullPage screenshot for interaction observation and guess UI from headings |
| Organize the storyboard by feature names and choose a suitable presentation for each | Mechanically repeat a title, screenshot, and identical slide-in for every feature |
| Proceed once sufficient evidence exists, capturing more as needed | Keep browsing unrelated content to meet a page quota |

If pages are inaccessible or require login, record the covered and uncovered scope and stop repeatedly visiting the same failing entry point. Continue work where existing assets suffice. If key facts still lack evidence, narrow the presentation or ask a specific question. Respect a user's restriction to supplied materials. Clearly label illustrative visuals, and do not treat editing compression as performance evidence.
