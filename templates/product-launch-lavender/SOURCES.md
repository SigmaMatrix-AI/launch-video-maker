# Sources and reuse boundaries

The four `assets/mock/*.png` files are generated, fictional Forma webpage illustrations, not captured SaaS pages. [source-manifest.json](records/source-manifest.json) records their generation purpose and hashes. They do not establish real product capabilities or customer endorsements.

The two `assets/fonts/PlusJakartaSans-*.woff2` files identify Plus Jakarta Sans version 2.071, Copyright 2020 The Plus Jakarta Sans Project Authors. They are licensed under SIL OFL 1.1; keep [PlusJakartaSans-OFL.txt](assets/fonts/PlusJakartaSans-OFL.txt) with the fonts. The notice comes from the [official Google Fonts distribution](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/plusjakartasans/OFL.txt).

`assets/audio/reference.wav` is a 31-second reference track. Its original author, source URL, and redistribution license are not recorded. The film uses it at gain 0.75. Obtain permission covering both video use and distribution of the WAV with an editable template, or replace it; the project's Apache-2.0 license and the font's OFL license do not cover this music. Replacement also requires updating the template's source-audio validation assertions and checking audiovisual timing.

Project-authored code and documentation follow the repository's Apache-2.0 license. The pinned runtime archive predates the explicit license metadata and has no LICENSE inside it; include the repository LICENSE when redistributing that archive independently. Retained technical validation records do not establish asset permission.
